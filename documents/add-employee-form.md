# Add Employee Form Redesign

## Overview

- Redesign the add employee form to streamline the flow of adding employee into the system and also the new payroll module + fixing some UX issues.

## What the product does, and who the audience is

- Add employee form is the first step of onboarding where the admin populates the employee details into the database and invites her to the software.
- **Main audience**: the admin or HR manager

## Business objectives and what needed to be achieved through design

- fix long-standing UX issues
- to reduce completion time and increase customer satisfaction, leading to word of mouth marketing

## Role & Timeline

- **My Role**: research, wireframing, interaction design, UI design
- **Duration**: 3 months
- **Cross-Functional Team**: 1x Designer, 2x PMs, 4x Devs, 2x QAs

## The design process

- Gathered the feedback and issues to fix, then played with the flow to discover other issues;
- No customer research: based on customer feedback provided by co-founder and customer success team
- Mapped out user journey to see we can fit in the new step to enroll employee to payroll co
- explored different layout and interaction for every step

## Challenges you faced in the project & how you overcame them?

- tried combining 2 jobs into 1 step, but it pushed down the impt job of assigning onboarding tasks, so we break them to 2 distinct steps.
- at the last summary step, we wanted to allow direct editing of details w/o needing to go back to the various steps. not technically possible, so the edit button brings them back to that particular step to edit.
- it's very complex technically to jump back to last step after editing, so decided to just let user to step through to the last step.
- wanted to try pulling in employee data from ATS app. timeboxed to explore the interaction and flow, but couldn't agree on final form, plus its a big rabbit hole, so we decided to push to later iteration.

## Reflections

- What you'd do differently:
  - all steps in single view and the detailed work is done in drawer modals.
- What the system still can't do cleanly:
  - allow editing of info at the last step
  - jumping across steps if those steps are completed
